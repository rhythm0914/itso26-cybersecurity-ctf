IT Skills Olympics 2026

Cybersecurity Category CTF

A team-based capture-the-flag platform. Register an account, build or join a team of three, solve 19 graded challenges across four official areas, and climb the scoreboard before the 90-minute clock runs out.

How it works

1. Create an account

Sign up with a username, email and password. Verify your email to unlock team features.

2. Build or join a team

Create a team and share the invite code, or search teams and submit a join request. Captains approve or reject requests.

3. Solve challenges

Work through 19 graded challenges with hints, attempts and a live scoreboard. Real-time team chat keeps you coordinated.

4. Compete

The captain starts the 90-minute clock. Highest score wins; ties broken by earliest last solve.
